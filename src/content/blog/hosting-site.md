I still remember when WordPress used to be all the rage. Those were _the days_ as some would put it. The overall thought around having a website or a blog has changed a lot since then.

Having gone through this rite of passage of
```
Blogger --> Wordpress --> CSS/HTML hosted static site(s) --> Hugo on Firebase --> ... this?
```

#### Why have your own website in the first place? What's the intrinsic value here?

I'm a big believer in an evidence of impact style of doing things. Having an outlet to comprehensively document, and reflect back like one would with a compendium, is something pretty neat.

And then there's the tighter control over the content, layout and overall end result when I am managing the entire end-to-end deployment. There's something about the utility of tinkering which has always been a big draw.  Not to say this is anything too drastic (simple Astro template with minor changes and hosted on Vercel)

#### What are you going to write about?

Mostly about things I'm experiementing with on my machine, learning recently or general observations over time. No specific theme per say.
